## License as stated by the rights holder

> eBible.org: "The World English Bible is in the Public Domain. That means that it is not copyrighted. However, \"World English Bible\" is a Trademark of eBible.org. You may copy, publish, proclaim, distribute, redistribute, sell, give away, quote, memorize, read publicly, broadcast, transmit, share, back up, post on the Internet, print, reproduce, preach, teach from, and use the World English Bible as much as you want, and others may also do so. All we ask is that if you CHANGE the actual text of the World English Bible in any way, you not call the result the World English Bible any more."

**Conditions:** A compilation that changes WEB wording must not be called the World English Bible. Each edition is imported as its own translation under its official abbreviation (WEB for Classic `eng-web`, WEBU for `engwebu`, WEBP for `engwebp`; sources.md §3.1).
