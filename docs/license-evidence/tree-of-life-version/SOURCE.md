# Tree of Life Version (TLV): license evidence

**Verdict:** Wanted, permission needed ([sources.md](../../sources.md) §4.1). Not obtained: no text has been downloaded.
**Evidence gathered:** 2026-10-25 (NZ time).

## License as stated by the rights holder

Copyright holder: the **Messianic Jewish Family Bible Society, Inc., DBA Tree of Life Bible Society** (a US 501(c)(3) charity). The quotation terms below are as published on Bible Gateway's TLV version page (copyright tab). The society's own website (tlvbiblesociety.org) has no permissions page: `/permissions` returns "The page you requested does not exist", and the site rate-limited automated requests (HTTP 429).

> "Copyright © 2014 by the Messianic Jewish Family Bible Society (DBA: Tree of Life Bible Society)."

> "The TLV text may be quoted in any form (written, visual, electronic, or audio) up to and inclusive of two hundred and fifty (250) verses without express written permission of the publisher, providing the verses quoted do not amount to a complete book of the Bible nor do the verses quoted account for 25 percent or more of the total text of the work in which they are quoted."

> "Notice of copyright must appear on the title or copyright page of the work as follows: Scripture taken from the Holy Scriptures, Tree of Life Version*. Copyright © 2014 by the Tree of Life Bible Society. Used by permission of the Tree of Life Bible Society."

> "'TLV' and 'Tree of Life Version' and 'Tree of Life Holy Scriptures' are trademarks registered in the United States Patent and Trademark office by the Messianic Jewish Family Bible Society (DBA Tree of Life Bible Society)."

> "When the quotation from the TLV is used in non-salable media, such as faith community bulletins, orders of service, posters, multi-media presentations and evangelistic outreach tools, a complete copyright notice is not required but the initials TLV must appear at the end of each quotation."

> "Quotations and/or reprints in excess of two hundred and fifty (250) verses, or other permission requests, must be directed to, and approved in writing by, the Tree of Life Bible Society. Use of additional content, including pictures and introductions, always require advance written permission."

**What this means for us:** hosting the full text exceeds the 250-verse allowance, so it needs written permission from the Tree of Life Bible Society. The society's site offers a contact page (`/pages/contact-us`) but no rights contact.

## Versification and canon

From the Bible Gateway book list (in the raw HTML; the list is a separate tab, not in the PDF):
- **Tanakh book order:** Torah, Prophets, Writings (Ruth, Daniel and Chronicles come after Malachi; 2 Chronicles closes the Old Testament).
- **Hebrew chapter numbering:** Joel has 4 chapters and Malachi has 3. Verse-level numbering (for example the Psalm titles) wasn't checked.
- 66 books, no deuterocanon.

This would make it the first translation in the collection with genuine Hebrew numbering.
