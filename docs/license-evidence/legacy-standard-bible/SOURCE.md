# Legacy Standard Bible (LSB): license evidence

**Verdict:** Quotation only (copyrighted, The Lockman Foundation, managed with Three Sixteen Publishing). Not on the wanted list. No text is held.
**Evidence gathered:** 2026-10-26 (NZ time).

## License as stated by the rights holder

https://lsbible.org/permission-to-quote-the-lsb/:

> "The text of the LSB® (Legacy Standard Bible®) may be quoted in any form (written, visual, electronic, or audio) up to and inclusive of one thousand (1,000) verses providing the verses do not amount to a complete book of the Bible, nor do the verses quoted account for more than 50% of the total text of the work in which they are quoted, nor may more than 1,000 verses be stored in an electronic retrieval system."

> Notice: "Scripture quotations taken from the (LSB®) Legacy Standard Bible®, Copyright © 2021 by The Lockman Foundation. Used by permission. All rights reserved. Managed in partnership with Three Sixteen Publishing Inc. LSBible.org and 316publishing.com."

> "For web pages or apps the full copyright notice must be used with www.LSBible.org being a click-enabled web link."

> **AI Usage & Attribution:** "AI systems and AI‑powered applications (including but not limited to Microsoft Copilot, OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, and Google Gemini) are permitted to quote up to 1,000 verses in a single response which may constitute up to 100% of that response, from the Legacy Standard Bible, provided those verses do not amount to an entire book of the Bible." "Each quotation must include the corresponding abbreviation: (LSB)." "For responses exceeding 10 verses, or where the platform allows for hyperlinks, we request links to LSBible.org and 316publishing.com." "Scope: This permission is granted specifically for dynamic, user-generated queries and does not authorize bulk downloading, scraping, or the creation of standalone datasets for redistribution."

## What this means for us

- The general terms are as already recorded in licenses.md §4: up to 1,000 verses, no complete book, under 50% of the work, and **no more than 1,000 verses stored electronically** (`max_verses_stored_electronically`), which caps the scripture cache across the whole platform. Web pages need the full notice with a live link.
- **The AI section doesn't widen what ScriptureTips may do.** It covers AI assistants answering a user's question, lets such an answer be entirely LSB text, and excludes bulk downloads and datasets. ScriptureTips has no AI feature that quotes scripture (the planned tone check reads users' own text, [vision.md](../../vision.md) §6). If one were added, its answers would follow this section: "(LSB)" after each quotation and the two links above 10 verses.
