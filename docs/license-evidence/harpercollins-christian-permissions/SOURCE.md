# HarperCollins Christian Publishing: permissions policy

**What this is:** the publisher-wide permissions page of HarperCollins Christian Publishing (Thomas Nelson, Zondervan, Harper Christian Resources). It isn't a text we obtain. It's kept because its rules apply to several translations at once and feed the license engine. Summary: [licenses.md](../../licenses.md) §4.1.
**Evidence gathered:** 2026-10-25 (NZ time).

## Translations covered

| Policy | Translations |
|---|---|
| **Thomas Nelson Bible quotation policy** | NKJV, NCV, ICB, NET (commercial use only), The Expanded Bible, The Voice |
| **Zondervan Bible quotation policy** | NIV (commercial use in North America), NIrV (commercial use in North America, UK & Commonwealth, EEC and EFTA) |

## Key wording (rights holder's own words)

> Thomas Nelson: "If your use falls under all of the guidelines listed below, you do not require written permission […] No more than 500 verses quoted in total. Scripture does not make up more than 25% of the total text. Scripture does not account for more than 50% of an entire book of the Bible. Scripture is not quoted in a commentary or any other Biblical Reference work. Scripture is properly cited […] Your product is not listed in the 'Not Covered by Gratis Use' section below." "If more than 50% of the Bible is being reproduced, regardless of verse count, written permission is required."

> "Not covered by Gratis Use: Scripture on a product in which the verse stands alone, such as artwork, note cards, crafts, novelty products, and jewelry. Use of charts, maps, illustrations, footnotes, study notes, and reference material from our study Bibles. Use of scripture in lyrics and musical compositions. Use of more than 50% of an entire book of the Bible or the complete Bible translation."

> "For non-commercial (meaning you will not be selling or receiving donations for your product) use of the NET, please refer to this page: https://netbible.com/faq/"

> Zondervan: "Text from the NIV or NIrV Bible may be quoted in any form (written, visual, electronic, or audio), up to and inclusive of 500 verses or less without written permission, providing the verses quoted do not amount to more than 50% of a complete book of the Bible, nor do verses quoted account for 25% or more of the total text of the work in which they are quoted, and the verses are not being quoted in a commentary or other biblical reference work."

> "Zondervan is granting permission for the latest edition of the NIV text only (currently 2011) […] We are not granting permission for use of the earlier editions of the NIV text."

> Whole translations and digital products: "Please contact our licensing department to find our [sic] more information about using one of our Bible translations in your smartphone application or other digital product."

The page also sets out the copyright notice for each translation, territory routing for the NIV and NIrV (Biblica outside North America; Hodder & Stoughton for commercial use in the UK, EEC and EFTA), church use, and photocopying.
