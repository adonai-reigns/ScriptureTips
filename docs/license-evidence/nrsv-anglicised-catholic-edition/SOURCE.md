# New Revised Standard Version, Anglicised Catholic Edition (NRSVACE): license evidence

**Verdict:** **Deferred** (2026-10-26). Not pursued for now: every NRSV edition needs written permission to host the full text, and the NRSV is closed to new licenses except the NRSV-CE. Research kept for reference. Not obtained: no text has been downloaded. Comparison of the NRSV editions: [sources.md](../../sources.md) §4.2.
**Evidence gathered:** 2026-10-25 (NZ time).

## License as stated by the rights holder

Copyright holder: the **National Council of the Churches of Christ in the USA (NCC)**. Its subsidiary Friendship Press publishes the permission policy; requests go to **Petradi Rights Management**.

Bible Gateway's NRSVACE version page (copyright tab):

> "New Revised Standard Version Bible: Anglicised Catholic Edition, copyright © 1989, 1993, 1995 the Division of Christian Education of the National Council of the Churches of Christ in the United States of America. Used by permission. All rights reserved."

> "Quotations or reprints more than five hundred (500) verses must be approved in writing by the National Council of Churches of Christ in the U.S.A., 110 Maryland Ave., Suite 108, Washington, DC 20002. Visit the website of their subsidiary, Friendship Press, at https://friendshippress.org/bible-licensing for current permission policy and contact information."

Friendship Press, "Licensing and Permission Guidelines" (`/bible-licensing` redirects to `/pages/nrsvue-quick-faq`):

> "The following usage guidelines apply to all versions of the RSV, NRSV, and NRSVue bible translations. (Note: Except for the NRSV-CE, the NRSV is no longer available for new licenses or permission agreements.)"

> "Up to 500 verses may be quoted in any form (written, visual, or audio) without charge and without obtaining written permission provided all of the following conditions are met: […] less than an entire book of the Bible, and less than 500 verses (total) […]"

> "The Fair Use Guidelines do not apply to Phone Applications, or other New Media Platforms (web sites, apps, etc.)."

> "Use of the text on a website is subject to the Gratis Use Policy described above. When calculating the number of verses, include all verses that can be accessed from the website. Website usage requires special permission or a license if they exceed the gratis use policy. Generally, gratis permission will not be granted to post or podcast: The complete text / An entire testament / A complete book of the Bible"

> "Mobile apps, downloadable book or Bible, and software are examples of digital usages that will require a special permission or license."

> Requests: "Petradi Rights Management, 2500 East Beltline Ave SE, Suite G, Box 352, Grand Rapids, MI 49546. Email: NCCrights@petradirights.com". Requests should state the translation, the verses or books, the working title, the proposed use, "if your usage will be salable, whether your organization is a non-profit […]".

The authorized notices list "New Revised Standard Version Bible: Anglicized Edition, copyright © 1989, 1995" and "New Revised Standard Version Bible: Catholic Edition, copyright © 1989, 1993", but no separate notice for the Anglicised Catholic Edition.

**What this means for us:**
- Hosting the full text needs a license from the NCC through Petradi.
- **The NRSV is closed to new licenses except the NRSV-CE.** Whether the Anglicised Catholic Edition counts as "NRSV-CE" isn't stated. Ask Petradi.
- **Alternatives if it doesn't:** the NRSVue (2021) is licensed, including a "NRSVue British Text" and a Catholic edition (the NRSVue Catholic Edition received a USCCB imprimatur in October 2025, per secondary sources; not captured).
- **Conflicting contact:** a search result for the licensee site `nrsvace.friendshippress.org` named Riggins Rights Management (`NRSVcopyright@rigginsrights.com`). That site refused connections on 2026-10-25, so it isn't captured. The live Friendship Press page names Petradi; treat Petradi as current.

## Versification and canon

From the Bible Gateway book list (in the raw HTML; the list is a separate tab, not in the PDF):
- **Catholic order with the deuterocanon placed among the books:** Tobit after Nehemiah, 1 Maccabees after Esther, Sirach after Wisdom, Baruch after Lamentations.
- **English chapter numbering:** Joel has 3 chapters and Malachi has 4.
- **Esther has 10 chapters** with the Greek additions included (how they're numbered, for example lettered chapters A–F, wasn't checked). **Daniel has 14 chapters** (Susanna as 13, Bel and the Dragon as 14).
