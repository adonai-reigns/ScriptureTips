# Open English Translation (OET): license evidence

**Verdict:** Licensable (CC BY-SA 4.0, share-alike), but **not ready**: the translators don't yet recommend it for Bible sites. No files are held. Not the same text as the Open English Bible ([open-english-bible](../open-english-bible/), CC0).
**Evidence gathered:** 2026-10-26 (NZ time).

## License as stated by the rights holder

OET licence page (https://OET.Bible/About/Licence):

> "The Bible translation and this website are each separately licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0) licence."

> "The Open English Translation of the Bible is copyright © 2010-2026 Freely-Given.org."

> Publication guidelines: "our copyright and attribution should be displayed in your publication or on your website, e.g., \"This Open English Translation of the Bible is copyright © 2010-2026 by Freely-Given.org and used under the terms of the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0) licence. The source files are available at https://OpenEnglishTranslation.Bible and at https://GitHub.com/Freely-Given-org/OpenEnglishTranslation--OET and were downloaded on such-and-such-a-date.\"" "If you wish to change or adapt the OET, you can also do that except that now you must change its name, e.g., \"This work was adapted from the Open English Translation of the Bible […]\"" "Because the OET licence is a 'share-alike' licence, you must also make your derivative work available on the same terms."

> Future plans: "as soon as any translated Bible book has been published at or above v1.0 for two years, that book is to be placed into the public domain. Or alternatively, upon the death of the principal translator or two years of inaction on the project repository (whichever is soonest), the entire work is to be placed into the public domain."

The repository README agrees: "Our OET Bible text is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0" and "Any included scripts are licensed GPL 3.0 or later."

**Status page** (https://OET.Bible/About/Status): "currently at v0.48". "Recommended for reading, Bible study, and for preaching from", but "Not yet recommended for inclusion on Bible sites (until we complete work on some consistency issues)", "Not yet recommended for memorisation", "Not yet recommended for mass printing". The README's goal is v0.5 by the end of 2027.

**What this means for us:**
- Credit with the wording above, including the download date (`attribution`).
- Works that include OET text carry CC BY-SA 4.0 (`share_alike`).
- An altered OET text must not be called the OET (`name_restriction`): a compilation that changes OET verses presents them as "adapted from the Open English Translation".
- Because of the status page, the OET is held back until the translators recommend it for Bible sites ([sources.md](../../sources.md) §8 item 10).
