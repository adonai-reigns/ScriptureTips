# NET Bible: license evidence

**Verdict:** **Ready, give away only.** The terms permit copying and printing the NET for others as long as it is given away, so ScriptureTips may host it and users may print free copies. This depends on ScriptureTips never being monetized ([vision.md](../../vision.md) §1.1). Editions users sell may contain it only within HarperCollins's gratis limits (see below).
**Evidence gathered:** 2026-10-25 (NZ time); edition check and change lists 2026-10-26.

## License as stated by the rights holder

> netbible.com/copyright (Biblical Studies Press): "The NET Bible® Scripture text (without the NET Bible notes) may be quoted in any form (written, visual, electronic, projection, or audio without written permission. This permission is contingent upon the quoted text being followed by the designation (NET) and an appropriate copyright acknowledgment: Scripture quoted by permission. Quotations designated (NET) are from the NET Bible® copyright ©1996, 2019 by Biblical Studies Press, L.L.C. http://netbible.com All rights reserved"

> "You may copy the NET Bible® and print it for others as long as you give it away, do not charge for it and comply with our guidelines for content control including current valid copyright and organizational acknowledgments. In this case, free means free. It cannot be bundled with anything sold, used as a gift to solicit donations, nor can you charge for shipping, handling, or anything."

> "When quotations from the NET Bible® are used in mobile apps, youtube channels, free apps, Internet apps or not-for-sale media […] The abbreviation (NET) must be used at the end of the quotation. For software apps with internet access the term NET must be hyperlinked to http://netbible.org."

> Commercial publication: "Please contact HarperCollins Christian Publishing". Anything not granted: permissions, Biblical Studies Press.

**Conditions:**
- text only, without the notes;
- "(NET)" after quotations, linked to netbible.org in software;
- the copyright acknowledgment;
- free means free: no sale, no bundling with anything sold, no charges of any kind (not even printing or postage passed to the recipient), and never offered as a gift for donating;
- "NET Bible®" is a registered trademark. Avoid "New English Translation Bible" or plain "New English Translation".

The bible.org site's own copyright statement covers bible.org articles. It refers to netbible.com/copyright for the NET.

**Content control guidelines:** the free-copy permission requires compliance with "our guidelines for content control including current valid copyright and organizational acknowledgments". The page doesn't define these guidelines beyond the acknowledgments, and no other published guidelines were found, so we comply by showing the current copyright and organizational acknowledgments.

**Copyright year:** the license asks for the "current valid copyright" acknowledgment, which is "©1996, 2019" on netbible.com and on the HarperCollins page. The eBible.org file carries "© 1996-2016". We show the current "©1996, 2019" acknowledgment, as the license asks.

## Commercial use and the meaning of "non-commercial" (HarperCollins)

netbible.com is now run by Thomas Nelson Bibles (HarperCollins Christian Publishing): the site's contact details and the NET FAQ say so. The copyright stays with Biblical Studies Press. The copyright page sends commercial publication to HarperCollins, whose permissions page covers the NET "(commercial use only)" under its Thomas Nelson Bible quotation policy. Captured in [`../harpercollins-christian-permissions/`](../harpercollins-christian-permissions/); summary in [licenses.md](../../licenses.md) §4.1.

> "For non-commercial (meaning you will not be selling or receiving donations for your product) use of the NET, please refer to this page: https://netbible.com/faq/"

The FAQ it points to (captured here) repeats the quotation permission and sends commercial publication back to HarperCollins.

**What this means:** together with Biblical Studies Press's "free means free" (no sale, no charges, never "used as a gift to solicit donations"), any money coming into the platform would make its use of the NET commercial. That is why ScriptureTips is never monetized ([vision.md](../../vision.md) §1.1).

**Commercial gratis use (HarperCollins):** up to 500 verses in total, no more than 25% of the work, no more than 50% of any book of the Bible, not in a commentary or other biblical reference work, properly cited. Above that, written permission is needed. This is what applies to editions for sale.
