# The Message (MSG): license evidence

**Verdict:** Wanted, permission needed. **Online use isn't covered by fair use:** any online use needs Tyndale's Permissions Questionnaire, so the NLT's online rules don't apply ([sources.md](../../sources.md) §4.1). No text is held, and there's no source route.
**Evidence gathered:** 2026-10-26 (NZ time).

## License as stated by the rights holder

Copyright holder: **Eugene H. Peterson** (© 1993, 2002, 2018); published by **NavPress**, represented by **Tyndale House Publishers**. From https://www.tyndale.com/permissions, section "THE MESSAGE":

> "The Message is not a study Bible, but rather a reading Bible. It is a version of Scripture translated by Eugene H. Peterson and published by NavPress Publishing Group. The verse numbers, which are not in the original documents, have been left out of the print version to facilitate easy and enjoyable reading."

> FAIR USE: "The text of The Message may be quoted in print or eBook form, up to and inclusive of 500 verses without express written permission of the publisher, provided that the verses quoted do not account for more than 25 percent of the work in which they are quoted, and provided that a complete book of the Bible is not quoted." Requests beyond that go to permission@tyndale.com or the Permissions Questionnaire.

> ONLINE PLATFORMS/AUDIO: "If you wish to use The Message for any online/audio production, please fill out our Permissions Questionnaire to discuss next steps."

> FURTHER GUIDELINES: "All material quoted from The Message must be done so accurately, including punctuation, capitalization, italics, etc. Tyndale must be made aware of any intended deviation in style, in writing, prior to publication." "If you have been granted permission for use of text from The Message, you may not pass permission on to another person."

Credit lines: "All Scripture quotations are taken from The Message, copyright © 1993, 2002, 2018 by Eugene H. Peterson. Used by permission of NavPress. All rights reserved. Represented by Tyndale House Publishers." (MSG only). With several translations, **"each verse quoted must be followed by (The Message)"** plus "Scripture quotations marked MSG are taken from…". Non-salable media: "(The Message) must appear at the end of each quotation." Artwork needs the Permissions Questionnaire.

**What this means for us:** print and eBook fair use matches the NLT (500 verses, 25%, never a complete book), but there's no online allowance. Showing MSG text anywhere on ScriptureTips, including compare view and notes, needs permission through the Permissions Questionnaire. In mixed works, every MSG verse carries "(The Message)" after it.

## Versification and canon

The print edition has no verse numbers. Bible Gateway shows MSG with verse numbers; how the text is divided into verses wasn't checked (no text held).
